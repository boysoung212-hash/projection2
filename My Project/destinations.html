Quick Planning Note

Travelers often combine Phnom Penh with Siem Reap, then add one slower stop such as Kirirom, Koh Rong, Kep, or Phnom Kulen for a less rushed itinerary.

For Short Trips

Focus on Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, and one nearby add-on like Tonle Sap or Banteay Srei.

For Nature Days

Choose Bokor, Kirirom, or Phnom Kulen when you want forest air, road views, and cooler stops.

For Coastal Travel

Mix Kep Beach, Koh Tonsay, or Koh Rong for sea views, seafood, and slower overnight stays.

Destination List

Browse the full collection of city, heritage, island, and countryside destinations in one place.

Phnom Penh

The Royal Palace of Cambodia

A must-visit attraction in Phnom Penh with Khmer architecture, gardens, and cultural history at the center of the capital.

Koh Rong

Koh Rong

Cambodia's second-largest island near Sihanoukville, known for bright water, beach stays, and a slower tropical pace.

National Museum

A strong stop in Phnom Penh for Khmer sculpture, historical artifacts, and a calmer city visit between major landmarks.

Phnom Penh
Kratie

Kratie Dolphin River

A calmer Mekong stop known for river views, boat rides, and chances to spot Irrawaddy dolphins outside the busier tourism loop.

Phnom Penh

Phnom Penh Riverside

A city stop for riverfront walks, local markets, and capital landmarks when you want a broader Phnom Penh view beyond palace and museum visits.

Kep

Kep Beach

A smaller coastal getaway with easy beach time, fresh seafood, and a gentler pace for short relaxing trips in the south.

Siem Reap

Banteay Srei Temple

A pink sandstone temple known for detailed carvings and a more intimate heritage stop outside the biggest Angkor crowds.

Kep

Koh Tonsay

Also called Rabbit Island, this short boat ride from Kep offers calm beaches, simple seaside stays, and a quieter coastal rhythm.

Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat

Located in Siem Reap, this temple complex is one of the country's most important historical landmarks and a core stop for visitors.

Kampot

Bokor National Park

A highland retreat in Kampot province with cooler air, scenic roads, and broad views that break away from the coast and city heat.

Kampong Speu

Kirirom National Park

A quieter mountain destination with forest scenery, lodge stays, and day-trip routes for travelers wanting a break from busier tourist centers.

Siem Reap

Tonle Sap Lake

A strong add-on from Siem Reap for floating-village scenery, lake travel, and a different look at daily life on Cambodia's largest freshwater lake.

Battambang

Battambang Bamboo Train

A popular countryside experience for travelers who want rural scenery, slower overland stops, and something more local than the usual city landmark route.

Preah Vihear

Preah Vihear Temple

A dramatic hilltop temple known for long approaches, historic stone structures, and wide views for travelers focused on heritage and remote landmarks.

Siem Reap

Phnom Kulen National Park

A forested plateau with waterfalls, river carvings, and a cooler day-trip mood for travelers wanting nature alongside temple routes.

Kampong Thom

Sambor Prei Kuk

An early temple complex with brick towers, shaded forest paths, and a quieter heritage experience between Phnom Penh and Siem Reap.
